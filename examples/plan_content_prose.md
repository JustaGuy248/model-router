# Content Marketing Sprint

This week's plan is written as prose to exercise the prose fallback parser.

We need to extract the top keywords from last quarter's analytics export. Then
summarize the three best-performing blog posts into a short recap. After that,
write a new pillar article on model routing for a technical audience. We should
also draft five social posts to promote it. Create a landing page for the lead
magnet and its email opt-in copy. Design a novel content-distribution strategy
across the newsletter, community, and paid channels from scratch. Finally,
reformat the old posts to the new style guide and rename their slugs.
